03 / A little about me

Security taught me to look for the edges.

My professional background includes enterprise information security, identity administration and operational automation. That experience left me with a healthy respect for permissions, audit trails, failure modes and the small details that can become big problems.

These days, I'm putting that perspective into software. I enjoy understanding a problem properly, working through architecture, and building in small steps that can be tested and explained. I'm still learning, and I like keeping the learning visible.
